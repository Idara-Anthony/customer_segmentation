# Customer Segmentation using Unsupervised Machine Learning


## 📌 Project Overview
This project applies unsupervised machine learning techniques to segment retail mall customers based on their annual income and spending behaviors. By identifying distinct customer personas, businesses can design targeted marketing strategies, optimize promotional campaigns, and improve overall customer retention.

The notebook explores data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, optimal cluster determination, and evaluates two distinct clustering algorithms, alongside anomaly detection to filter out noise.

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset used is the **Mall Customers Dataset**, which contains structural data on 200 mall clients.
* **Source:** Kaggle / Synthetic retail tracking data.
* **Key Features:**
  * `CustomerID`: Unique ID assigned to the customer.
  * `Gender`: Male or Female.
  * `Age`: Age of the customer.
  * `Annual Income (k$)`: Annual income of the customer in thousands of dollars.
  * `Spending Score (1-100)`: Score assigned by the mall based on customer behavior and spending nature.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
* **Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation:** `pandas`, `numpy`
* **Data Visualization:** `matplotlib`, `seaborn`
* **Machine Learning (Scikit-Learn):**
  * `StandardScaler` (Feature Scaling)

## 🚀 Workflow & Methodology

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Inspected the distribution of core numerical attributes (`Age`, `Annual Income`, `Spending Score`).
* Identified initial linear and non-linear patterns through distribution plotting.

### 2. Feature Engineering & Scaling
* Selected non-demographic behavioural features: `Annual Income` and `Spending Score`.
* Applied `StandardScaler` to ensure the algorithms treat both axes with equal variance weights.

### 3. Outlier Detection
* Implemented an **Isolation Forest** anomaly model with a 5% expected contamination rate.
* Isolated 10 global outlier profiles from the core 190 typical customer paths to improve cluster boundary definitions.

### 4. Clustering Implementations
* **K-Means Clustering:** Leveraged the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS) "Elbow Method" to evaluate distances up to $K=20$. The optimal threshold was selected at **5 clusters**. Evaluated with a Silhouette Score of **~0.55**.
* **Hierarchical Clustering:** Plotted a Dendrogram using Ward's linkage method to visually confirm the natural cluster partitions, verifying 4 core operational groupings.

## 💡 Customer Segment Insights

Based on the target center-means generated by the models, the market can be segmented into 5 highly actionable profiles:

| Segment ID | Segment Profile Name | Behavioral Characteristics | Strategic Business Action |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | **Middle of the Road** | Average income and moderate spending habits. Form the baseline revenue bulk of the mall. | Retain with broad engagement campaigns and standard newsletter offers. |
| **1** | **VIP / Loyalists** | High annual income paired with a high spending score. | Induct into premium loyalty/VIP rewards tiers with exclusive product access. |
| **2** | **Impulsive Buyers** | Low income but exceptionally high spending score. | Target with flash sales, immediate discounts, and trendy low-ticket items. |
| **3** | **High-Income Careful Spenders** | High annual income but ultra-conservative spending habits. | Pitch high-value investments, product guarantees, or highly utility-focused promotions. |
| **4** | **Under-resourced** | Low income and low spending tendencies. | Avoid high ad-spend; target strictly with absolute value and budget propositions. |
